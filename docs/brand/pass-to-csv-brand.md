## Approved Soft Grove extensions

- Destination cards use the primary fill (Brass `#D9A55B` in Ember Charcoal; formerly Olive `#6B8F71`) for the selected state, with an explicit checked indicator; Fern remains a focus accent rather than the selected fill. Cards receive Soft Grove layout and weight treatment, not only a color change.
- Dark-mode glass/glow is an approved Warm Grove extension. Retain the device-controlled theme, restrained surfaces, and readable contrast; no separate theme toggle.

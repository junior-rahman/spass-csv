# Play Store listing — Spcc

**Title:** Spcc
**Price:** Paid app — USD $2.99 one-time (lifetime). No subscription, no ads, no in-app purchases. Set in Play Console → Monetize → App pricing (local prices auto-converted).
**Subtitle / short description:** Spass to CSV Converter — Samsung Pass export converter, on-device, nothing uploaded.

**Full description:**
Spcc (Spass to CSV Converter) turns your Samsung Pass export into a CSV you can import into 1Password, Google Password Manager, Apple Passwords, Bitwarden, or any other password manager.

- Converts on your phone. Nothing is uploaded. The only network use is Google Play In-App Updates; conversion stays offline.
- Destinations: 1Password, Google Password Manager, Apple Passwords, Bitwarden, or a generic CSV for anything else.
- Never invents websites or passwords, and never overwrites a real website from your export. Missing titles, usernames, or websites show up under Needs a look so you can fix them before saving.
- If two logins share the same website (or app) and the same username, you can merge them and pick which fields to keep.
- Asks before saving, walks you through import, and reminds you to delete the CSV afterward.

Not affiliated with Samsung.

**Data safety:** No data collected. No data shared. Data is not encrypted in transit because none is transmitted.
